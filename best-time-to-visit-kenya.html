Best Time to Visit Kenya: Safari Seasons and Migration Dates

There is no bad month for a Kenyan safari, only months that cost three times as much as others. Here is the trade-off, laid out.

July to October is the best time for a Kenyan safari, because the Masai Mara holds the great migration and the bush is dry enough to see through. It is also the most expensive window of the year. January and February are the best compromise, dry and cheaper, and April and May are the cheapest months by a wide margin.

The migration is a continuous movement, not an event with a date. Herds are generally in the Mara from around July to October, but river crossings depend on rainfall and cannot be booked.

The great migration in Kenya

Around two million wildebeest, zebra and gazelle move in a continuous loop between the Serengeti in Tanzania and the Masai Mara in Kenya, following the rain. The Kenyan leg is roughly July to October.

The Mara River crossings are the part everyone comes for, when herds mass on the bank and eventually plunge across into water holding very large crocodiles. They happen most often between late July and September, but they are triggered by grazing and rainfall, not by a calendar. Some days there are several, some days none.

Anyone who guarantees you a river crossing on a specific date is selling something they cannot deliver. Give yourself three or four days in the Mara during the window and your odds are good.

Best time for a safari, by what you want

  • The migration and river crossings: late July to September. Book camps six to twelve months ahead.
  • Best general game viewing: January and February, when the bush is thin and animals concentrate at water.
  • Lowest prices: April and May, when camps discount hard and the parks are almost empty.
  • Best value overall: November, or early June before peak pricing starts.
  • Birdlife: November to April, when migrants are present.
  • The coast: December to March for heat and sun, July to October for cooler and dry.

What the seasons do to your bill

Camp rates in the Mara can move by a factor of two or three between the April low and the August peak, for the same tent. Nairobi and the coast move far less, because their demand is not driven by the migration. That means the cheapest way to shift a Kenyan trip's cost is to move the safari days, not the city or beach days.

The rains, honestly

The long rains from April into May are the reason those months are cheap, and they are far less disruptive than most people expect. Rain usually comes as a heavy afternoon downpour rather than all-day drizzle, mornings are often clear, and the landscape is green rather than dust brown. What you lose is easy visibility, because the grass is high and animals are dispersed, and some smaller camps close entirely.

The short rains in November are lighter again and rarely affect a trip beyond a wet hour or two in the afternoon. November is the single most underrated month in the Kenyan calendar.

When to book

  • July to September travel: book six to twelve months ahead. The good Mara camps sell out.
  • December and January: three to six months ahead, because of the holiday peak.
  • April, May and November: a month or two is usually plenty.

Common questions

What is the best time to visit Kenya?

July to October for the great migration in the Masai Mara, which is also the most expensive window. January and February are excellent for general game viewing at lower prices, and April and May are the cheapest months of the year.

When is the great migration in the Masai Mara?

Roughly July to October. The Mara River crossings are most frequent between late July and September, but they depend on rainfall and grazing rather than a fixed date, so allow three or four days in the reserve to see one.

Is it worth visiting Kenya in the rainy season?

Yes, if price matters. The long rains in April and May usually mean a heavy afternoon downpour rather than all-day rain, the parks are nearly empty and camp rates fall sharply. The trade-off is high grass and dispersed animals.

What is the cheapest month to visit Kenya?

April or May. Camp rates in the Masai Mara can be a half or a third of the August peak for the same tent. November is the best balance of low prices and settled weather.

How far ahead should I book a Masai Mara camp?

Six to twelve months for July to September travel, because the best camps sell out. Three to six months for the December holidays. One or two months is enough for April, May and November.
