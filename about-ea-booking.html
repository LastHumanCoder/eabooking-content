About EA Booking: East Africa Hotels and Stays at Low Prices

An online travel agency built around East Africa, where the inventory is real, the prices include the taxes, and the guides are written by people who checked the rates.

EA Booking, also known as East Africa Booking, is an East Africa online travel agency. We sell hotel and resort stays, car rental and activities across Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Ethiopia and the wider region, with prices shown inclusive of taxes and fees.

To be clear about what we do not do: flights are not currently available through EA Booking. Hotels and activities are live today.

Where we focus

East Africa is the centre of what we do, and the guides on this site reflect that rather than treating the region as an afterthought behind Europe and North America.

  • Kenya: Nairobi, the Masai Mara, Mombasa and Diani Beach.
  • Tanzania: Arusha, the Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Moshi for Kilimanjaro, and Zanzibar.
  • Uganda: Kampala, Entebbe, Fort Portal for Kibale, and Mbarara.
  • Ethiopia: Addis Ababa and the northern historic route through Lalibela, Gondar and Bahir Dar.
  • The Gulf and Turkey: Dubai and Istanbul for trade and shopping trips, and Makkah and Madinah for Umrah.

How we are different from a generic booking site

  • Real inventory, quoted honestly. Every price in our guides comes from the live booking engine, not from a brochure. Where a destination has thin inventory we say so, and where we have none at all we say that too rather than pretending otherwise.
  • Prices include taxes and fees. The figure you see is the figure you pay, which is not universal in this industry.
  • The guides link to our own inventory. If a page tells you a Nairobi room costs $22, there is a link next to it that shows you the room.
  • Regional depth. We cover Fort Portal, Mbarara, Bahir Dar and Mwanza, not only the capitals.

Common questions

What is EA Booking?

An online travel agency focused on East Africa. We sell hotel and resort stays, car rental and activities across Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Ethiopia and the wider region, with prices shown inclusive of taxes and fees.

Can I book flights on EA Booking?

Not at present. Hotels, resort stays, car rental and activities are live today. Flights are not currently part of the booking engine, so they need to be arranged separately.

Which countries does EA Booking cover?

Kenya, Tanzania including Zanzibar, Uganda and Ethiopia are the core. We also cover the Gulf and Turkey routes East African travellers use most, including Dubai, Istanbul, Makkah, Madinah, Jeddah, Riyadh and Doha.

Are the prices shown final?

Prices are displayed inclusive of taxes and fees, so the total you see is what you pay us. Individual properties may charge local levies such as a tourism tax on arrival, and their own terms always apply.

Is EA Booking the same as ebooking.com or eBookers?

No. EA Booking is unrelated to ebooking.com, to eBookers, and to the events company trading under a similar name. We operate only at eabooking.com.
