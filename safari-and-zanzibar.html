Safari and Zanzibar Trip: Best Combos, Costs and Itinerary

Safari days are the most expensive days of any East Africa trip. Beach days are among the cheapest. That arithmetic is why almost everyone pairs them.

Short answer

The standard combination is four or five days in the Serengeti and Ngorongoro followed by five to seven nights on Zanzibar, and it works because the beach half costs a fraction of the safari half. A Serengeti camp starts around $53 a night and runs past $600, while a Zanzibar beach hotel starts around $25 and a good mid-range one sits near $100.

Serengeti and Zanzibar

This is the pairing most people mean. Both are in Tanzania, so there is no second visa and no border crossing, and the internal flight from the Serengeti airstrips to Zanzibar is routine. It is also the version with the widest price range, because Serengeti accommodation runs from about $53 at a gateway hotel to well over $4,000 at the top camps.

Do the safari first. Arriving at the beach with the game drives behind you is much better than the reverse, when you spend the beach days thinking about the early starts to come.

Ngorongoro and Zanzibar

If you only have a short safari window, Ngorongoro delivers more animals per hour than anywhere else in the region, because the crater is a contained ecosystem and the game does not migrate out of it. Two nights on the rim plus a full crater day is enough to see a great deal.

The constraint is supply. There are around six bookable properties, from about $194 to $1,750 a night, and they fill. Book this before anything else in the trip.

Masai Mara and Diani

The Kenyan version, and the cheapest of the three. The Mara is the best big cat viewing in East Africa and Diani on the south coast is the best value beach in the region, with rooms from about $22. Everything stays inside one country, one visa and one domestic flight network.

If your dates fall between roughly July and October, this pairing also puts you in the Mara for the migration river crossings. See best time to visit Kenya.

What the whole trip costs

For two people sharing, on a mid-range budget, a ten day safari and beach trip typically lands between $3,000 and $6,000 per person excluding international flights, and the spread inside that comes almost entirely from which safari camps you choose. The beach half rarely moves the total much.

How many nights on each side

Four safari nights is the point where the trip stops feeling rushed: one to arrive, two full game days, one to move on. Three works if you are only doing Ngorongoro. Beyond five, most people find the game drives start to blur, and the money is better spent on the coast.

On the beach, five nights is the minimum that feels like a holiday rather than a stopover, and seven is the sweet spot. Splitting those between two beaches rarely pays: the transfer eats half a day and the islands' beaches differ less than the photographs suggest.

Practical notes

  • Safari first, beach second. Always. The early starts belong at the front of the trip.
  • Book Ngorongoro before anything else. Six properties is not many.
  • Zanzibar tides decide your beach. Nungwi and Kendwa in the north stay swimmable at any tide. Paje and Jambiani in the east do not.
  • Pack for two climates. Cold mornings on the crater rim, humid heat on the island.
  • Flights are separate. EA Booking sells hotels, resort stays, cars and activities. Internal flights are arranged elsewhere.
  • Two countries means two visas if you pair Kenya with Tanzania. See our visa guide, and note Tanzania sits outside the joint East Africa visa.

Common questions

How many days do you need for a safari and Zanzibar trip?

Ten days is the comfortable minimum: four or five on safari and five on the beach, plus arrival and departure. Seven days works if you do Ngorongoro only. Fourteen lets you add Tarangire and more beach time without rushing.

Should I do the safari or Zanzibar first?

Safari first. Game drives mean early starts and long days in a vehicle, and the beach is a much better way to end a trip than to begin one.

How much does a safari and Zanzibar trip cost?

Roughly $3,000 to $6,000 per person for ten days on a mid-range budget, excluding international flights. Safari camps drive nearly all the variation. Zanzibar hotels start around $25 a night and rarely change the total much.

Can I combine a Kenyan safari with Zanzibar?

Yes, but it means two countries and two visas. If you want one country, pair the Masai Mara with Diani Beach in Kenya, or the Serengeti with Zanzibar in Tanzania. The Kenyan version is usually the cheaper of the two.

Which Zanzibar beach suits a post-safari week?

Nungwi or Kendwa in the north, where the sea is deep enough to swim at any tide. Paje is cheaper and better for kitesurfing, but the tide retreats a long way during part of each day.
