Diani Beach Hotels: Where to Stay on Kenya's South Coast

Diani is the best value beach week in East Africa: white sand, a reef that keeps the water calm, and rooms from $22 a night within walking distance of it.

Short answer

Stay on the central beach strip near Diani Beach Road for the best mix of sand, restaurants and price. Diani has around 70 bookable properties on EA Booking, from about $22 a night for a cottage to $390 for a five star beachfront resort, with the median around $140.

Unlike much of the Indian Ocean coast, the reef means you can swim at most states of the tide. That is the main reason Diani beats the alternatives for a first coastal trip.

Safari from the coast

You do not have to fly back to Nairobi to see wildlife. Tsavo East and Tsavo West together form one of the largest protected areas in Africa and both are reachable from the south coast as an overnight or two-night trip. Shimba Hills National Reserve is closer still, about an hour inland from Diani, and is the easiest half-day wildlife outing on this coast.

A coast-plus-Tsavo trip is considerably cheaper than a Masai Mara safari, because the lodges cost less and the park fees are lower. Our African safari cost guide compares the two properly. Park fees for Tsavo and Shimba Hills are published by the Kenya Wildlife Service, and should be confirmed before you budget.

Mombasa as an alternative base

Mombasa is about an hour north of Diani, across the Likoni ferry, and is much cheaper: around 237 properties with a median of about $51 a night. It suits a trip built around Old Town, Fort Jesus and the coast road rather than a beach week. For sand, Diani is better. For price and city interest, Mombasa wins.

What to check before you book a beach hotel

Three things decide whether a Diani room is good value, and none of them is the star rating. First, how far back from the sand it actually sits: "beach access" can mean a private gate or a ten-minute walk across a road. Second, whether the rate includes breakfast, because at the lower end it often does not and that changes the daily total more than the room price does.

Third, seaweed. Parts of this coast collect it seasonally, and it affects some stretches far more than others. It is worth asking the property directly which months are affected on their section of beach rather than relying on photographs, which are never taken in the weeks it is there.

Ukunda airstrip sits just inland from the beach and takes domestic flights from Nairobi. The alternative is to fly or take the train to Mombasa and drive down, which involves the Likoni ferry and can be slow at peak times. Allow more time than the distance suggests.

When to come

December to March is hot, dry and busy, and it is the peak for prices. July to October is cooler and dry, and is the best overlap with safari season. April and May are the long rains, when rates fall hard and some smaller places close. See best time to visit Kenya.

Getting around once you are there

Tuk-tuks run the length of the beach road and cost very little, which makes staying a few minutes back from the sand far more practical than it sounds. Agree the fare before you get in.

Common questions

How much are hotels in Diani Beach?

From about $22 a night for a cottage set back from the sand, with mid-range beach hotels between $100 and $200 and five star beachfront resorts around $280 to $420. The median across the beach is roughly $140.

Is Diani better than Mombasa for a beach holiday?

Yes, for the beach itself. Diani has the better sand and a reef that keeps the water calm and swimmable. Mombasa is cheaper, with a median around $51 a night, and better if you want the Old Town, Fort Jesus and city life.

Can you go on safari from Diani?

Yes. Shimba Hills National Reserve is about an hour inland and works as a half or full day trip. Tsavo East and Tsavo West are an overnight or two-night trip from the coast and cost much less than a Masai Mara safari.

When is the cheapest time to visit Diani?

April and May, the long rains, when rates drop sharply. November is the best compromise between price and weather. December to March is the most expensive.

Can you swim at Diani at low tide?

Usually yes. The reef lies about a kilometre offshore and keeps a lagoon of calm water, so Diani is far less affected by tides than the east coast of Zanzibar. Seaweed can gather on parts of the beach at certain times of year.
