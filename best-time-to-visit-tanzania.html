Best Time to Visit Tanzania: Weather and Prices by Month

Tanzania's migration is in the country almost all year. The question is not when to come, it is which part of the Serengeti to be in when you do.

Short answer

June to October is the classic Tanzania safari season, dry and reliable, and it is peak price. But the migration is in Tanzania for around ten months of the year, so January and February are arguably better: the calving season in the southern Serengeti, excellent predator action, and lower rates than the June to October peak.

For Zanzibar, avoid April and May, which are the long rains and when many smaller hotels close.

The calving season is the underrated answer

Between late January and February, around half a million wildebeest calves are born in the southern Serengeti in a matter of weeks. It draws every predator in the ecosystem, which makes it the best period of the year for lion, cheetah and hyena action, and the herds are concentrated rather than strung out across hundreds of kilometres.

It is also cheaper than the July to September peak and far less crowded. If your priority is wildlife rather than seeing a specific river crossing, this is the better window.

River crossings

The Grumeti and Mara river crossings happen in the northern Serengeti, mostly between July and September. As in Kenya, they are driven by grazing and rain rather than a schedule. Base yourself in the northern Serengeti for three or four nights during the window to give yourself a realistic chance.

The same herds cross into Kenya's Masai Mara during these months, which is why the two countries compete for the same peak season. See best time to visit Kenya.

Hot air balloon over the Serengeti

Balloon flights run year round in the Serengeti, launching at dawn and typically followed by a bush breakfast. They operate in the sector where the camps are, so the part of the park you are in changes with the season. Conditions are most reliable in the dry months from June to October and in January and February. Flights are weather dependent and can be cancelled on the morning.

Zanzibar, which runs on a different calendar

The island's seasons do not match the mainland's.

  • June to October: dry, breezy, reliable. The best overlap with safari season.
  • December to February: hot, calm, the peak beach season and the highest prices.
  • March to May: the long rains. Cheapest by far, and many smaller hotels close in April and May.
  • November: short rains, still good, and very good value.

How far ahead to book

For July to September travel, book six to twelve months out. The northern Serengeti camps are few and they fill, and Ngorongoro has only a handful of properties at any time. For the January and February calving window, three to six months is usually enough. For April, May and November you can often book a month out and still choose freely, which is part of what makes those months good value.

Kilimanjaro has its own windows

The best months to climb are January to mid March and June to October. The long rains in April and May make the trails miserable and the summit views unreliable. January and February are usually warmer and quieter than the July to September rush. Costs are in our Tanzania trip cost guide.

What the rains actually do

The long rains in April and May usually arrive as a heavy afternoon downpour rather than all-day drizzle, and mornings are often clear. What you lose is easy visibility, because the grass is high and the animals are dispersed, and some smaller camps close entirely. The short rains in November rarely cost you more than an hour of an afternoon.

A sample itinerary by season

  • January or February: Arusha, Ngorongoro, southern Serengeti for calving, then Zanzibar.
  • July to September: Arusha, Ngorongoro, northern Serengeti for crossings, then Zanzibar.
  • April, May or November: Tarangire, Ngorongoro, central Serengeti, then Zanzibar, at the lowest prices of the year.

Common questions

What is the best time to visit Tanzania?

June to October is the classic dry safari season and the peak for prices. January and February are arguably better value, with the calving season in the southern Serengeti, excellent predator action and lower rates.

When is the best time to see the great migration?

The migration is in Tanzania for most of the year, so it depends what you want. Late January and February for calving in the southern Serengeti, and July to September for river crossings in the north.

When are the Serengeti river crossings?

Mostly July to September on the Grumeti and Mara rivers in the northern Serengeti. They are driven by rainfall and grazing rather than a schedule, so allow three or four nights in the north during the window.

Can you take a hot air balloon over the Serengeti year round?

Yes, flights operate all year, launching at dawn from the sector where the camps are. Conditions are most reliable in the dry months from June to October and in January and February, and flights are weather dependent.

When is the cheapest time to visit Tanzania?

April and May, the long rains, when camp and hotel rates fall sharply. November is the best compromise, with short rains, low prices and the herds moving back into the eastern Serengeti.
