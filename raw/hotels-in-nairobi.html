Hotels in Nairobi: Best Areas, Airport Stays and Cheap Picks

Nairobi is a city where the neighbourhood matters more than the star rating. Here is what each area costs, who it suits, and where the genuinely cheap rooms are.

Short answer

Stay in Westlands for restaurants and nightlife, the CBD for the cheapest central rooms, Kilimani or Upper Hill for business, and near JKIA only if you have an early flight. Nairobi has more than 600 bookable properties on EA Booking, with rooms from about $17 a night and the median around $64.

The city is large and traffic is heavy, so booking in the wrong area can add an hour to every journey. Pick the area first, then the hotel.

Westlands

Westlands is where most first-time visitors end up, and usually should. It concentrates the restaurants, bars, malls and coworking space into a walkable few blocks, it is close enough to the CBD for meetings, and it avoids the worst of the city-centre congestion. Expect to pay a premium of roughly twenty to thirty percent over the CBD for the same standard of room.

The CBD

The central business district has the cheapest central rooms in Nairobi by a clear margin, with a median around $56 a night and solid mid-range hotels under $70. It is busy, functional and well connected by matatu and bus. It is not where you go for a quiet evening, and most visitors prefer not to walk it late at night, but for value and access it is hard to beat.

Upper Hill and Kilimani

Upper Hill is Nairobi's corporate district, home to banks, headquarters and a cluster of business hotels. Kilimani next door is more residential, leafier and full of apartments and guest houses. Both suit a working trip where you want a calm room at the end of the day and do not need nightlife on the doorstep. Rates sit between Westlands and the CBD.

Hotels near JKIA airport

Jomo Kenyatta International is around 20 kilometres from the city centre, which can mean twenty minutes or well over an hour depending on traffic. An airport hotel is worth it for a pre-dawn departure or a long layover, and rarely worth it otherwise, because you will spend the whole stay commuting.

Practical notes before you book

  • Traffic decides your day. Nairobi congestion is severe between about 7 and 9.30 in the morning and 4.30 and 7.30 in the evening. Book near where you will actually spend your time.
  • Rates usually include taxes. Prices on EA Booking are shown inclusive of taxes and fees, so the figure you see is the figure you pay.
  • Serviced apartments are good value. In Westlands and Kilimani especially, an apartment often costs less than a hotel room of the same standard and comes with a kitchen.
  • Book early in the high season. July to October and December fill up, especially anything near the parks.

Common questions

What is the best area to stay in Nairobi?

Westlands for most visitors, because it has the restaurants, malls and bars in a walkable area and reasonable access to the rest of the city. Choose the CBD if price matters most, Upper Hill or Kilimani for business, and Karen if you want green space.

How much does a hotel in Nairobi cost?

Rooms start around $17 a night and the median is about $64. A comfortable mid-range hotel in Westlands or the CBD is typically $55 to $90, and the top international hotels run $150 to $225.

Is it worth staying near JKIA airport?

Only for a very early departure or a long layover. The airport is around 20 kilometres from the centre and traffic is unpredictable, so an airport hotel saves a stressful morning but costs you a day in the city.

Are there hostels in Nairobi?

Is Nairobi safe for visitors?

Ordinary city precautions apply. Use taxis or ride hailing at night rather than walking, keep phones and bags out of sight in crowds, and prefer Westlands, Kilimani, Upper Hill or Karen if you would rather not think about it. Check your own government's current travel advice before you travel.
