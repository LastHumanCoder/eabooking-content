Tanzania Travel Guide: Best National Parks and Entry Fees

Tanzania holds the Serengeti, the Ngorongoro Crater, Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar. Here is what each one costs, where to base yourself, and how the fees actually work.

Short answer

The Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area are Tanzania's two essential parks, and they are run by different authorities with separate fees. Base yourself in Arusha for the northern circuit, where hotels start around $18 a night, or Moshi for Kilimanjaro, from about $17.

Park fees are charged per person per 24 hours on top of everything else, and descending into the Ngorongoro crater carries an additional vehicle fee.

Park entry fees and how they are charged

Tanzania splits responsibility between two bodies, which is the single most common source of budgeting mistakes.

  • National parks including the Serengeti, Tarangire, Lake Manyara and Kilimanjaro are managed by TANAPA. Entry is charged per person per 24 hours, with different rates for non-residents, residents and children, and camping or concession fees on top if you sleep inside the park.
  • The Ngorongoro Conservation Area is not a national park. It is managed separately by the Ngorongoro Conservation Area Authority and charges its own entry fee. Descending into the crater itself carries an additional service fee levied per vehicle, not per person.
  • Kilimanjaro climbs carry park fees, rescue fees and hut or camping fees for every day on the mountain, which is why a climb costs what it does before guides and porters are counted.

Both authorities revise their tariffs periodically and publish them on their own sites. Confirm the current figure there before you budget. A fee quoted in an old blog post is the most common reason a Tanzania budget comes up short.

Where to stay for the northern circuit

Arusha

Arusha is the gateway to the Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire and Manyara, and almost every northern safari starts here. It has around 150 bookable properties with a median of about $63 a night, which makes it the cheapest sensible base in northern Tanzania.

Inside the Serengeti and on the Ngorongoro rim

Sleeping inside the parks saves hours of driving and costs a great deal more. Serengeti properties run from about $53 for a gateway hotel to well over $4,000 for the top camps, and Ngorongoro has only a handful of properties bookable at any time, from around $194 to $1,750. Book the Ngorongoro leg first, because it is the constraint.

Beyond the northern circuit

Zanzibar

The island is the standard second half of a Tanzania trip and by far the cheaper half. Over 400 properties from about $25 a night. Full detail on our Zanzibar hotels page, and we cost the safari plus beach pairing on safari and Zanzibar.

Dar es Salaam

The commercial capital and the gateway to the southern parks and the ferry to Zanzibar. Around 133 properties with a median near $70 and rooms from about $24.

Mwanza

On the southern shore of Lake Victoria and the back door to the western Serengeti. Inventory here is thin, with only around a dozen bookable properties, so book early if this is part of your route.

Backpacking Tanzania

It is very doable. Arusha, Moshi and Stone Town all have rooms under $30, buses connect the main towns cheaply, and the real expense is the park fees rather than the beds. Join a group safari to split the vehicle, travel in April, May or November, and put more of the trip on Zanzibar, where a day costs a fraction of a day in the Serengeti.

Planning

Most first trips run ten to fourteen days: a northern circuit safari of four or five days from Arusha, then a week on Zanzibar. Best time to visit Tanzania covers the migration month by month, and East Africa tourist visa covers entry, including why Tanzania sits outside the joint visa.

Common questions

What are the best national parks in Tanzania?

The Serengeti for the migration and big cats, the Ngorongoro Conservation Area for the crater, Tarangire for elephants and baobabs, and Lake Manyara as an easy half day. Nyerere and Ruaha in the south are excellent and far quieter.

How much is the Serengeti entry fee?

It is charged per person for each 24 hours in the park and varies by season and residency, with camping and concession fees added if you sleep inside. TANAPA publishes the current tariff, and it should be confirmed there before you budget.

Are Ngorongoro fees separate from the national parks?

Yes. The Ngorongoro Conservation Area is managed by its own authority, not by TANAPA, so it charges a separate entry fee. Descending into the crater carries an extra service fee charged per vehicle.

Where should I stay for a Kilimanjaro climb?

Moshi, at the foot of the mountain, where rooms start around $17 a night. Almost all climbs begin and end there, with a night either side of the trek.

Is Tanzania good for backpackers?

Yes for accommodation and transport, where rooms under $30 and cheap buses are easy to find. The park fees are the expensive part and they do not vary by how you travel, so join a group safari to share the vehicle and spend more of the trip on Zanzibar.
