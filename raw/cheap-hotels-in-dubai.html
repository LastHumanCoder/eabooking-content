Cheap Hotels in Dubai: Deira, Bur Dubai and Airport Stays

Dubai has a reputation for costing a fortune. The old city on either side of the creek has never been expensive, and it is where most business travellers from East Africa actually stay.

Short answer

Stay in Deira for the cheapest rooms and the best access to the gold and textile souks, or Bur Dubai across the creek for a slightly quieter version of the same. Dubai has over 1,100 bookable properties on EA Booking, with rooms from about $32 a night in Deira and a city-wide median near $87.

Deira is also the closest district to Dubai International, which matters if you are connecting onward or flying in late.

Where the cheap rooms are

Cheap hotels in Dubai are concentrated on either side of the creek, in Deira and Bur Dubai, rather than out along the beach and the Marina.

Deira

Deira sits on the northern side of Dubai Creek and is the oldest commercial part of the city. It is where the gold souk, the spice souk and the textile wholesalers are, it is dense with small hotels at every price point, and it is roughly ten to fifteen minutes from Dubai International.

For anyone travelling to buy goods, connect onward, or simply spend as little as possible on a bed, this is the right district. Rooms start at about $32 and there is a deep run of well-run three and four star properties between $70 and $120.

Bur Dubai

Bur Dubai is across the creek, reachable by abra water taxi for a few cents, and holds the Al Fahidi historical district, the textile souk and the Dubai Museum. It is a little calmer than Deira and the mid-range is stronger, with rooms from about $51 and a median near $158.

Hotels near Dubai airport

Dubai International sits at the edge of Deira, which makes the whole district effectively an airport hotel zone at normal city prices rather than airport prices. For a short connection or a late arrival, a Deira hotel gives you a real room in a real neighbourhood, ten minutes from the terminal, for less than a dedicated airport hotel usually costs.

The metro's red line runs from both terminals through Deira and Bur Dubai and is by far the cheapest way in.

How to keep the cost down

  • Travel in summer. May to September is brutally hot and rates collapse. If your trip is indoors, in souks, malls and offices, this is the value window.
  • Stay on the creek, not the beach. Deira and Bur Dubai are a half or a third of Marina and Downtown prices.
  • Use hotel apartments for longer stays. Dubai has a deep apartment market and they beat hotel rooms beyond three or four nights.
  • Use the metro. It runs from the airport through both old districts and costs very little.
  • Check what is included. Tourism fees per room per night are standard in Dubai. Prices on EA Booking are shown inclusive of taxes and fees.

Istanbul, the other half of the same trip

A lot of travellers comparing Dubai are also comparing Istanbul, because both work as trade and shopping destinations with good onward connections. Istanbul has over 1,000 bookable properties from about $39 a night, which makes it cheaper than Dubai at almost every level.

Laleli is the district that matters for this kind of trip. It is the wholesale textile quarter, walking distance from the Grand Bazaar, and hotels there run about $53 to $216 a night with a median near $115.

Practical notes

  • Flights are booked separately. EA Booking sells hotels, resort stays, car rental and activities.
  • Check your visa. UAE entry rules vary by nationality, and some passports get visa on arrival while others need one in advance. Confirm with the UAE authorities before booking.
  • Summer heat is not a figure of speech. June to September routinely passes 40 degrees. Plan around air conditioning.
  • Ramadan changes the day. Eating and drinking in public during daylight is restricted, and business hours shift.

Getting in from the airport

The metro's red line runs from both terminals through Deira and Bur Dubai and costs a few dirhams, which is the cheapest arrival in the city.

Common questions

Where are the cheapest hotels in Dubai?

Deira, on the north side of the creek, where rooms start around $32 a night and good three and four star properties run $70 to $120. Bur Dubai across the water is slightly more expensive and a little quieter.

Is Deira a good place to stay in Dubai?

Yes, if you want value, the souks and easy access to the airport. It is the oldest commercial district, dense with hotels at every price, and ten to fifteen minutes from Dubai International. It is not the place for beach or nightlife.

Are there cheap hotels near Dubai airport?

Yes. Dubai International sits at the edge of Deira, so the whole district works as an airport zone at city prices. A Deira hotel is usually better value than a dedicated airport hotel, and the metro red line connects both terminals.

When is Dubai cheapest?

May to September, when temperatures routinely pass 40 degrees and rates fall sharply. If your trip is indoors, in souks, malls and meetings, summer is the value window. November to March is peak.

Is Istanbul cheaper than Dubai?

Generally yes. Istanbul has over 1,000 bookable properties from about $39 a night against Dubai's median near $87. For a trade or shopping trip, the Laleli district near the Grand Bazaar runs about $53 to $216.
