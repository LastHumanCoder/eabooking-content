Ethiopia Tours: Lalibela, Gondar and Where to Stay in 2026

Ethiopia is not a safari country and does not try to be. It is the oldest continuously inhabited place most travellers will ever visit, and the northern historic route is why people come.

The core Ethiopia trip is the northern historic route: Lalibela for the rock-hewn churches, Gondar for the castles, and Bahir Dar for Lake Tana and the Blue Nile Falls. It is also remarkably cheap. Hotels in Lalibela start around $17 a night, Bahir Dar from $21 and Addis Ababa from $16.

Before you plan, check your own government's current travel advice for Ethiopia, because conditions vary by region and change.

The northern historic route

Almost all Ethiopia tours follow this loop, and for good reason: it holds the churches, the castles and the lake monasteries within one internal flight network.

Lalibela

Lalibela is the reason most people come to Ethiopia. Eleven churches were cut downward out of solid volcanic rock in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, and they are not ruins, they are working churches with daily services. You need two full days to do them properly, and a guide is worth paying for because the layout and the symbolism are not self-explanatory.

There are around 27 bookable properties, with a median around $31 a night, which is remarkable for a UNESCO World Heritage site of this standing.

Gondar

Gondar was the imperial capital for two centuries and the Fasil Ghebbi royal enclosure is unlike anything else on the continent, a walled compound of stone castles that looks transplanted from medieval Europe. It is also the staging point for the Simien Mountains.

Inventory here is genuinely thin, with only around four properties bookable at a time, so this is the leg to secure first.

Bahir Dar

Bahir Dar sits on Lake Tana, the source of the Blue Nile, and it is the most relaxed stop on the route. Boat trips run out to the island monasteries, several of which hold manuscripts and murals centuries old, and the Blue Nile Falls are a short drive away. Around 15 properties from about $21 a night.

Addis Ababa

Almost every trip starts in Addis, which is both the capital and the hub of Ethiopian Airlines, so it is where you connect to the northern towns. Around 178 properties from about $16 a night. Our Addis Ababa hotels page covers Bole, the airport and the guest house market in detail.

Ethiopia's national parks

Ethiopia's parks are about landscape and endemic species rather than the big five. The Simien Mountains are the headline, with gelada baboons, walia ibex and some of the most dramatic escarpment scenery in Africa, and the Bale Mountains in the south hold the Ethiopian wolf, the rarest canid in the world. The Danakil Depression in the north east is a different proposition again and needs organised logistics and current security advice.

Park fees and permits are set by the relevant authorities and change, so confirm them before budgeting. If you are comparing Ethiopia against a conventional safari, our African safari cost guide sets out the difference.

Backpacking Ethiopia

Ethiopia is cheap once you are there. Rooms under $30 are normal even in the historic towns, food is inexpensive, and domestic flights on Ethiopian Airlines are discounted when you fly into the country with them. The main practical constraints are distances, road conditions and the need to check regional travel advice as you plan.

When to go

October to March is the dry season and the main window. Timkat in January, the Orthodox Epiphany, is one of the great religious festivals anywhere and Gondar is its centre, which also makes it the hardest week of the year to find a room. The main rains run roughly June to September.

Before you book

  • Check current travel advice from your own government for the specific regions on your route.
  • Book the thin towns first. Gondar and Bahir Dar have very limited inventory compared with Addis.
  • Allow a buffer on internal flights. The northern route is normally flown rather than driven, and schedules can move.
  • Entry is by e-visa. See our East Africa tourist visa guide, which covers the Ethiopian e-visa and why Ethiopia sits outside the joint East Africa visa.

Common questions

What is the best Ethiopia tour route?

The northern historic route: Addis Ababa, then Lalibela for the rock-hewn churches, Gondar for the royal castles and the Simien Mountains, and Bahir Dar for Lake Tana and the Blue Nile Falls. Ten to fourteen days covers it comfortably.

How much are hotels in Lalibela?

From about $17 a night, with a median around $31. There are roughly 27 bookable properties, so book ahead, particularly around the January Timkat festival.

Are there hotels in Gondar?

Yes, but inventory is very limited, with only around four properties bookable at a time, priced from about $59 to $93 a night. Secure Gondar before the rest of your itinerary.

Does Ethiopia have national parks?

Yes, though they are about landscape and endemic species rather than big game. The Simien Mountains have gelada baboons and walia ibex, and the Bale Mountains hold the Ethiopian wolf. Confirm current fees and access with the relevant authorities.

When is the best time to visit Ethiopia?

October to March, the dry season. January brings Timkat, the Orthodox Epiphany, which is spectacular in Gondar and fills every room in town.
