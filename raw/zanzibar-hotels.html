Zanzibar Hotels: Best Beaches, Stone Town and Budget Stays

Zanzibar is not one destination. The beach you choose decides your tide, your nightlife and your bill, and the difference between them is bigger than most people expect.

Pick Nungwi in the north if you want swimmable sea at any tide, Paje in the east for kitesurfing and low prices, and Stone Town for history and the cheapest rooms on the island. Zanzibar has over 400 bookable properties on EA Booking, from about $25 a night in Stone Town to $800 for beachfront luxury.

The one thing to know before booking anywhere on the east coast: the tide goes out a long way, and for part of each day you will not be able to swim from the beach.

Stone Town

The old city is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the cheapest place to sleep on the island. Rooms start around $25 a night and there is a deep run of restored merchant houses turned boutique hotels between $80 and $130. You are not here for the beach, you are here for the carved doors, the Forodhani night market and the spice history. Two nights is usually right, either side of a beach stay.

Nungwi and Kendwa

The northern tip has the only beaches on Zanzibar where the sea stays deep enough to swim at every tide. That single fact explains why Nungwi is busier and slightly pricier than the east coast. It also has the island's best sunsets, the most bars and the widest choice of dive operators. If this is your first trip and you want to swim without checking a tide table, stay here.

Paje and the east coast

Paje is the kitesurfing capital of the island and the best value beach on it. The lagoon is shallow, turquoise and enormous at low tide, which is exactly what kiters want and exactly what disappoints people expecting to swim. Prices are noticeably lower than the north, the crowd is younger, and the beach bars are relaxed.

All-inclusive resorts

Zanzibar's all-inclusive market is concentrated on the north and north-east coasts, around Nungwi, Kendwa and Matemwe. Expect roughly $175 to $350 a night for a well-regarded four or five star all-inclusive, rising to about $800 at the top of the market. Below about $150 a night, an all-inclusive rate usually means a limited menu and local drinks only, which is fine if you know it going in.

Cheap places to stay in Zanzibar

The island has genuinely cheap inventory. Stone Town starts at about $25, Paje at $40 and Matemwe at $36. Guest houses and small apartments at that level are common and often a short walk from the beach rather than on it, which is where most of the saving comes from. Practical notes

  • Check the tide before you book the east coast. At Paje and Jambiani the water can retreat several hundred metres. Beautiful, but not swimmable at that point in the day.
  • The rains matter here. April and May are the long rains, when many smaller places close. June to October and December to February are the reliable months.
  • Zanzibar is conservative outside the resorts. Swimwear is for the beach. Cover shoulders and knees in Stone Town and the villages.
  • Most visitors combine it with a safari. The mainland parks and the island pair naturally, and we price that trip on our safari and Zanzibar page.

Common questions

Which is the best beach in Zanzibar?

Nungwi and Kendwa in the north, because the sea is deep enough to swim at any tide. Paje and Jambiani in the east are better for kitesurfing and cost less, but the tide goes out a long way. Matemwe is the quietest of the main beaches.

How much do Zanzibar hotels cost?

From about $25 a night in Stone Town and $40 at Paje, with comfortable mid-range beach hotels around $70 to $125 and four or five star all-inclusive resorts between $175 and $350. The top beachfront properties reach about $800.

Where should I stay in Zanzibar for the first time?

Two nights in Stone Town for the history and food, then four or five nights at Nungwi or Kendwa where you can swim whenever you like. That combination covers the island without a long transfer in the middle.

Are there cheap hotels in Zanzibar?

Yes. Stone Town has rooms from around $25 a night and Paje from about $40. Guest houses set back from the beach are the best value, and a short walk to the sand is usually where the saving comes from.

When is the cheapest time to visit Zanzibar?

April and May, the long rains, when rates fall sharply and some smaller hotels close. November is a good compromise, with lower prices than the December peak and mostly settled weather.
