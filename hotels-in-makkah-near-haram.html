Hotels in Makkah Near the Haram and Madinah Stays for Umrah

For Umrah, two things decide the hotel: how far you will walk to the Haram, and what that walk costs. Everything else is secondary.

Short answer

In Makkah, hotels in the Jabal Omar and Ajyad areas put you within a short walk of the Haram, and that proximity is what you are paying for. Rates run from about $45 a night for a four star a little further out to $245 to $307 for the five star towers beside the mosque, with a median near $225.

Madinah is considerably cheaper. Hotels near Masjid an-Nabawi start around $35 with a median near $86.

Hotels near the Haram in Makkah

Hotels in Makkah near Haram are concentrated in two areas, and the walk to an entrance is what you are paying for.

The Jabal Omar development and the Ajyad area sit immediately beside Masjid al-Haram, and the towers there are as close as accommodation gets. Being able to walk to the mosque in a few minutes, several times a day, is worth a great deal during Umrah, particularly for older pilgrims or anyone travelling with family.

Cheap hotels in Makkah

Rooms start around $30 a night, and the saving is almost entirely distance. A hotel fifteen or twenty minutes' walk out, or a short shuttle ride, can be a third of the price of a tower beside the mosque.

Whether that trade is right depends on your group. For a short Umrah trip with fit adults it usually is. For an extended stay, elderly pilgrims, or anyone intending to pray all five daily prayers at the Haram, paying for proximity is often the better decision, because the walk happens ten times a day rather than twice.

Check what a listing means by "near the Haram". Distances are sometimes given to the outer boundary of the complex rather than to an entrance, and the complex is very large. Look at the map view before booking.

Madinah, near Masjid an-Nabawi

Madinah is the calmer and considerably cheaper of the two cities. The hotel district wraps around the mosque in concentric rings, so the same proximity logic applies, but the prices are far gentler: around 148 properties with a median near $86 and rooms from about $35.

Jeddah, the arrival city

Most international pilgrims arrive at King Abdulaziz International in Jeddah, about 90 kilometres from Makkah. Jeddah has around 204 properties with a median near $101 and rooms from about $53, and a night here makes sense if you land late or leave early. It also has Al-Balad, the restored historic old town, if you have a spare day.

Riyadh and Doha

Riyadh has around 154 properties with a median near $126, and is a business rather than pilgrimage destination. Doha, with around 109 properties from about $38 and a median near $97, is a common connecting point on Qatar Airways routes and works well as a stopover city. Riyadh hotels and Doha hotels.

How long to stay in each city

Most Umrah itineraries run longer in Madinah than in Makkah, and the pricing is part of why: the median room there is roughly a third of the Makkah median. A common shape is three or four nights in Makkah for the rites, then four or five in Madinah at a calmer pace and a lower nightly rate. Booking them as two separate stays rather than one package almost always comes out cheaper.

Before you book

  • Check the distance on a map, not just the description. Walking time to an actual entrance is what matters.
  • Umrah visas and entry rules are set by the Saudi authorities and change. Confirm current requirements through official Saudi channels before booking anything.
  • Ramadan and Hajj are a different market entirely. Rates multiply and availability disappears months ahead. Nothing on this page reflects those periods.
  • Apartments suit families. Both cities have a deep serviced apartment market that beats multiple hotel rooms.
  • Prices include taxes. The figure shown on EA Booking is inclusive of taxes and fees.
  • Flights are separate. EA Booking sells hotels, resort stays, cars and activities.

Common questions

How much are hotels in Makkah near the Haram?

Five star towers in the Jabal Omar area beside the mosque run about $245 to $307 a night. Good four star options a little further out start around $45, and the city median is near $225. Rooms across Makkah start from about $30.

Is it worth paying more to stay close to the Haram?

Usually yes for a longer stay, elderly pilgrims or anyone praying all five daily prayers at the mosque, because the walk happens many times a day. For a short trip with fit adults, a hotel fifteen minutes out at a third of the price is a reasonable trade.

How much are hotels in Madinah near Masjid an-Nabawi?

From about $35 a night, with a median near $86. Madinah is significantly cheaper than Makkah, which is why most Umrah itineraries spend more nights there.

Should I stay in Jeddah before going to Makkah?

It is worth a night if you land late or depart early, since King Abdulaziz International is about 90 kilometres from Makkah. Jeddah rooms start around $53 with a median near $101.

Do these prices apply during Ramadan or Hajj?

No. Rates in both cities multiply during Ramadan and Hajj and availability goes months in advance. The figures here reflect an ordinary period and should not be used to budget for those seasons.
